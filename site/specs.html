Systems and processors

SystemVersionsProcessorsPackages
LinuxDebian 13, Ubuntu 24.04 and 26.04, Fedora 44, Arch; Wayland and X11x86_64, ARM64; RISC-V on Debian and Ubuntu.deb and .rpm for each release, PKGBUILD for Arch
WindowsWindows 10 and 11x64, ARM64Per-user installer (.msi), portable .zip
macOSmacOS 14 or laterApple SiliconInstaller package (.pkg)

Formats

ContainersEvery format FFmpeg reads, among them MP4, MOV, Matroska, WebM, AVI, MPEG-TS, MPEG-PS, FLV, WMV, Ogg, MXF and 3GP
VideoH.264, HEVC, AV1, VP9, VP8, MPEG-2, MPEG-4, ProRes, DNxHD, Theora and the rest FFmpeg decodes
SoundAAC, MP3, Opus, Vorbis, FLAC, ALAC, AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS, WAV, AIFF, WavPack, Monkey's Audio, Musepack, WMA and the rest FFmpeg decodes
SubtitlesSubRip, WebVTT, MP4 text and other text subtitles; ASS and SSA styled by libass; Blu-ray, DVD and DVB picture subtitles; subtitle files beside the video or in a Subs folder
PlaylistsM3U and PLS, files opened together, and optionally the opened file's folder
StreamsHTTP and HTTPS, HLS, DASH, RTSP, SRT
Web pagesYouTube's and the other sites yt-dlp reads, when it is installed, with playlists

Playback

DecodingOn the graphics card where it can: VAAPI, CUDA or Vulkan on Linux, Direct3D 11 on Windows, VideoToolbox on macOS, up to 8K at 60 frames a second; on the processor otherwise
ColorsBT.601, BT.709 and BT.2020, 8 to 16 bits; HDR10 and HLG tone mapped for ordinary screens; rotation from the file's metadata
Scalingplaa's own shader, filtering shrunk pictures as mpv does, so text and fine lines stay free of moiré
SoundIn step with the picture by the output device's clock; the output chosen in Settings, through PipeWire or PulseAudio on Linux
SeekingDragging the timeline with the picture following; 5 and 10 seconds; frame by frame; chapters; a preview over the timeline
SpeedFrom 0.25 to 3 times, keeping the pitch
LoopingThe file, or an A-B loop
ZoomUp to 16 times at the pointer, dragged to look around
ViewingOne window per file, full screen, picture in picture
VisualizerSpectrum, waveform, or Prism in three dimensions

Editing and export

EditingTrim, split into clips, arrange and remove clips, rotate, flip; undo and redo; nothing changes until you export
FiltersThrough agents and the assistant: FFmpeg's filters on the picture or sound, all of it or a part, such as black and white, color, crop, sharpening, titles, noise reduction and loudness; checked before they run, played at once and exported
Mixing filesThrough agents and the assistant: parts of other files put in, and another file's sound or picture, or silence or black, over a part; played and exported as edited, other pictures fitted in the movie's
Video exportH.264, HEVC or AV1 with AAC sound, at the file's size or smaller, as 720p or 480p; HDR kept in HEVC and AV1
EncodersThe graphics card's where one opens (NVENC, AMF, Quick Sync, VAAPI, VideoToolbox); x264, x265 and SVT-AV1 on the processor otherwise
Sound exportAAC, MP3 or Opus at a bit rate chosen; FLAC, ALAC or WAV in 16 or 24 bits
FramesThe picture shown, saved as a PNG

Recording

SoundA microphone, or what the computer plays: the sound server's monitors on Linux, every output on Windows and macOS. A level meter, up to 30 dB louder, levelling automatically, in the sound export's formats
ScreenA screen, a window or, on Linux, a region, with the pointer: through the ScreenCast portal and PipeWire on Linux, Windows.Graphics.Capture on Windows and ScreenCaptureKit on macOS
CameraThrough FFmpeg: V4L2 on Linux, DirectShow on Windows, AVFoundation on macOS, iPhones as Continuity Camera included
Video formatsH.264, HEVC or AV1 on the graphics card's encoder where one opens, at 24, 30, 60 or 120 pictures a second or every picture sent, with a microphone's sound or what the computer plays

Casting

DevicesChromecast, found through mDNS; TVs, speakers and programs that play DLNA (UPnP AV), found through SSDP
What goesA file, served by plaa from this computer, or a stream's address
ControlPlay, pause and seek from plaa's own controls and timeline

Network audio

RoleA Milan talker and listener of its own on Linux; on macOS, through the Mac's own AVB entity
Stream formatsAAF, as the AVB community's Basic and Extended Audio profiles set them, and AM824, of 2 or 8 channels, at 48, 96 or 192 kHz, in SR class A or B
ControlAny Milan controller connects plaa's stream; Network Audio connects listeners to it, and Connect plays a talker's stream on this computer
Without AVB switchesAVB Lite: a copy of the stream for each listener, up to five
TimegPTP from ptp4l, on an interface with a PTP hardware clock, such as Intel's I210
Beside triibShares the network's reservations with triib's entity service where it runs

Languages

Interface38 languages: Arabic, Bengali, Catalan, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Czech, Danish, Dutch, English (US and UK), Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Norwegian (Bokmål), Persian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Romanian, Russian, Spanish, Swahili, Swedish, Tamil, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, Urdu, Vietnamese
Right to leftArabic, Hebrew, Persian and Urdu lay out right to left
ChoosingFollows the system's language, or the one chosen in Settings; audio and subtitle tracks follow the languages Settings prefer
TranslationsOne Fluent file per language; the translations other than English are first drafts open to further review

Input

KeyboardShortcuts for every main action, with ⌘ on macOS and Ctrl elsewhere; changeable in the settings file, for every system or for one
Media keysPlay, pause, next and previous, through MPRIS on Linux, the media flyout on Windows and Now Playing on macOS
PointerMouse and touchpad: the timeline, Ctrl+wheel to zoom at the pointer, dragging to look around
Drag and dropFiles dropped on a window open in it, on Wayland as elsewhere
AccessibilityKeyboard focus with Tab and Shift+Tab; follows the system's reduced motion setting

Assistant and agents

Cloud modelsAnthropic, OpenAI and Google Gemini, with your own API key, kept in the system keychain
Local modelsOllama, or an OpenAI-compatible server such as LM Studio's, llama.cpp's or vLLM's, on this computer or another
What a model needsTool calling; seeing pictures is optional
Shared modelsSet up once for the Scramble Tools apps, such as prev, in scramble/assistant.toml in the config folder; each app chooses its own
MCP serverplaa --mcp, over standard input and output, for Claude Code (claude mcp add plaa -- plaa --mcp) or any MCP client. It drives the plaa you are using, starting it if needed; nothing listens on the network
Accessplaa asks before letting a new agent in, and remembers Allow; Settings lists allowed agents, and outside control can be turned off
Tools (33)list_windows, get_status, get_info, get_frame, play, pause, seek, set_speed, set_volume, set_loop, choose_tracks, next_item, previous_item, set_fullscreen, focus_window, open and close_window; get_frames, get_levels, get_subtitles and get_sound; get_edit, trim, cut, split, move_clip, insert, replace, apply_filter, remove_filters, turn_picture, undo and export
ReadingAny part of what plays, as edited: pictures at any times as JPEGs, how loud it is and where it is silent, the subtitles' text, and the sound itself for models that hear, such as Gemini
Asking firstA switch each for seeing what plays, controlling playback, opening files or closing windows, and editing or exporting

Technology

LanguageRust, edition 2024, Rust 1.95 or newer to build
Interfaceiced 0.14 with wgpu, and scramble-ui, shared with prev and triib
MediaFFmpeg 9 through ffmpeg-next; libass for styled subtitles; cpal for sound
Network audioThe atdecc, avb-lite, avb-mrp, avb-net and avb-stream crates; raw sockets through a small helper holding CAP_NET_RAW on Linux, and Apple's AudioVideoBridging framework on macOS
TranslationsProject Fluent
AI agentsAn MCP server through rmcp, relaying to the running plaa over a private local socket (a named pipe on Windows)
Assistantrig-core for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Ollama and OpenAI-compatible servers; API keys through keyring (the Secret Service on Linux, the Credential Manager on Windows, the Keychain on macOS)
FontsRoboto Flex and Material Symbols Rounded, bundled
System integrationXDG desktop portals and MPRIS on Linux; Win32 and the System Media Transport Controls on Windows; AppKit, MediaPlayer and Launch Services on macOS, with a menu bar through muda
DependenciesAll open source, with licenses checked by cargo-deny in CI

License and privacy

LicenseGNU GPL-3.0 or later; the bundled fonts keep their own licenses (SIL Open Font License 1.1, Apache 2.0)
Sourcegithub.com/scrambletools/plaa, with the sources of the libraries the Windows and macOS packages carry on each release
NetworkNone of its own: plaa goes online only to play the addresses and web pages you open and to open links you click; on your local network it finds TVs and speakers as the Cast menu opens, and takes part in the AVB network you choose. No accounts, no telemetry
AI agentsOnly those you allow, through plaa --mcp; no bundled model. What an agent sees goes to the model it uses
AssistantTalks only to the model you add: a local model stays on your computer, and a cloud model gets what it asks to see. API keys stay in the system keychain
Code signingWindows releases are signed by Scramble Tools LLC, and macOS releases with Scramble Tools' Developer ID and notarized by Apple; see the code signing policy
