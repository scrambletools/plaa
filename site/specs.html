Formats

ContainersEvery format FFmpeg reads, among them MP4, MOV, Matroska, WebM, AVI, MPEG-TS, MPEG-PS, FLV, WMV, Ogg, MXF and 3GP
VideoH.264, HEVC, AV1, VP9, VP8, MPEG-2, MPEG-4, ProRes, DNxHD, Theora and the rest FFmpeg decodes
SoundAAC, MP3, Opus, Vorbis, FLAC, ALAC, AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS, WAV, AIFF, WavPack, Monkey's Audio, Musepack, WMA and the rest FFmpeg decodes
SubtitlesSubRip, WebVTT, MP4 text and other text subtitles; ASS and SSA styled by libass; Blu-ray, DVD and DVB picture subtitles; subtitle files named after the video, beside it or in a Subs or Subtitles folder
PlaylistsM3U and PLS, files opened together, and optionally the opened file's folder
StreamsHTTP and HTTPS, HLS, DASH, RTSP, SRT
Web pagesYouTube's and the other sites yt-dlp reads, when it is installed, with playlists

Playback

DecodingOn the graphics card where it can: VAAPI, CUDA or Vulkan on Linux, Direct3D 11, CUDA or DXVA2 on Windows, VideoToolbox on macOS, up to 8K at 60 frames a second on a card that can; on the processor otherwise
ColorsBT.601, BT.709 and BT.2020, 8 to 16 bits; HDR10 and HLG tone mapped for ordinary screens; rotation from the file's metadata
Scalingplaa's own shader, filtering shrunk pictures as mpv does, so text and fine lines stay free of moiré
SoundThe picture kept in step with it by the output device's clock; the output chosen in Settings, through PipeWire or PulseAudio on Linux
SeekingDragging the timeline with the picture following; 5 and 10 seconds; frame by frame; chapters; a preview over the timeline
SpeedFrom 0.25 to 3 times, keeping the pitch
LoopingThe file, or an A-B loop
ZoomUp to 16 times at the pointer, dragged to look around
ViewingOne window per file, full screen, picture in picture
VisualizerSpectrum, waveform, Prism in three dimensions, or the cover alone

Editing and export

EditingTrim, split into clips, arrange and remove clips, rotate, flip; undo and redo; nothing changes until you export. Agents and the assistant make each edit too
Mixing filesThrough agents and the assistant: parts of other files put in, and another file's picture or sound, or silence or black, in place of a part's own; played and exported as edited, other pictures fitted in the movie's
Mixing in soundThrough agents and the assistant: another file's sound, all of it or a part, mixed in with what plays from a time of the edit, at -60 to +24 dB, with fades in and out; it keeps its time as clips change, plays at once and exports
FiltersThrough agents and the assistant: FFmpeg's filters on the picture or sound, all of it or a part, such as black and white, color, crop, sharpening, titles, fades, noise reduction, volume and loudness; checked before they run, and only filters that change each frame as it comes, none that touch files, devices or the network; played at once, undone with undo, and exported, a crop over the whole movie at its new size
Video exportH.264, HEVC or AV1 with AAC sound, at the file's size or smaller: 1440p, 1080p, 720p or 480p; HDR kept in HEVC and AV1
EncodersThe graphics card's where one opens (NVENC, AMF, Quick Sync, VAAPI, and VideoToolbox for H.264 and HEVC); x264, x265 and SVT-AV1 on the processor otherwise
Sound exportAAC, MP3 or Opus at a bit rate chosen; FLAC, ALAC or WAV in 16 or 24 bits
FramesThe picture shown, saved as a PNG

Recording

SoundA microphone, or what the computer plays: the sound server's monitors on Linux, every output on Windows and on macOS 14.2 or later. A level meter, up to 30 dB louder, levelling automatically, in the sound export's formats
ScreenA screen, a window or, where the Linux desktop offers one, a region, with the pointer: through the ScreenCast portal and PipeWire on Linux, Windows.Graphics.Capture on Windows and ScreenCaptureKit on macOS
CameraThrough FFmpeg: V4L2 on Linux, DirectShow on Windows, AVFoundation on macOS
Video formatsH.264, HEVC or AV1 on the graphics card's encoder where one opens and the processor's otherwise, at 24, 30, 60 or 120 pictures a second or every picture sent, with a microphone's sound or what the computer plays

Casting

DevicesChromecast, found through mDNS; TVs, speakers and programs that play DLNA (UPnP AV), found through SSDP
What goesA file as it is, without an edit, served by plaa from this computer, or a stream's address
ControlPlay, pause, seek and volume from plaa's own controls and timeline

Network audio

RoleA Milan talker and listener of its own on Linux; on macOS, a talker and listener through the Mac's own AVB entity; on Windows, neither
Stream formatsOn Linux, AAF as the AVB community's Basic and Extended Audio profiles set them, in SR class A or B, and AM824 in class A, of 2 or 8 channels at 48, 96 or 192 kHz, where a frame holds them (2 channels only in class B at 192 kHz); on macOS, the Mac's entity's: AAF of 8 channels at 44.1 to 192 kHz, and AM824 of 8 channels at 48, 96 or 192 kHz
ControlAny Milan controller connects plaa's stream; Network Audio connects listeners to it, and on Linux and macOS, with Send what plays on, Connect plays a talker's stream on this computer; both list only entities under plaa's gPTP grandmaster
Without AVB switchesOn Linux, AVB Lite: a copy of the stream for each listener, up to five
TimeOn Linux, gPTP from ptp4l, on an interface with a PTP hardware clock, such as Intel's I210; on macOS, the Mac's own
Beside triibOn Linux, shares the network's reservations with triib's entity service where it runs

Languages

Interface38 languages: Arabic, Bengali, Catalan, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Czech, Danish, Dutch, English (US and UK), Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Norwegian (Bokmål), Persian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil and Portugal), Romanian, Russian, Spanish, Swahili, Swedish, Tamil, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, Urdu, Vietnamese

TranslationsOne Fluent file per language; the translations other than English are first drafts open to further review

Input

KeyboardShortcuts for every main action, with ⌘ on macOS and Ctrl elsewhere; changeable in the settings file, for every system or for one
Media keysPlay, pause, next and previous, through MPRIS on Linux, the media flyout on Windows and Now Playing on macOS
PointerMouse and touchpad: the timeline, Ctrl+wheel to zoom at the pointer, dragging to look around
Drag and dropFiles dropped on a window open in it, on Wayland as elsewhere
AccessibilityFollows the system's reduced motion setting, and Settings can turn animations off

Assistant and agents

Cloud modelsAnthropic, OpenAI and Google Gemini, with your own API key, kept in the system keychain
Local modelsOllama, or an OpenAI-compatible server such as LM Studio's, llama.cpp's or vLLM's, on this computer or another
What a model needsTool calling; seeing pictures is optional
Shared modelsSet up once for the Scramble Tools apps, such as prev, in scramble/assistant.toml in the config folder; each app chooses its own
MCP serverplaa --mcp, over standard input and output, for Claude Code (claude mcp add plaa -- plaa --mcp) or any MCP client. It drives the plaa you are using, starting it if needed; nothing listens on the network
Accessplaa asks before letting a new agent in, and remembers Allow; Settings lists allowed agents, and outside control can be turned off
Tools (35)list_windows, get_status, get_info, get_frame, play, pause, seek, set_speed, set_volume, set_loop, choose_tracks, next_item, previous_item, set_fullscreen, focus_window, open and close_window; get_frames, get_levels, get_subtitles and get_sound; get_edit, trim, cut, split, move_clip, insert, replace, mix_in, remove_mix, apply_filter, remove_filters, turn_picture, undo and export
ReadingAny part of what plays, as edited: pictures at any times as JPEGs, how loud it is and where it is silent, the subtitles' text, and the sound itself for models that hear, such as Gemini
ProgressA step that takes more than two seconds shows its progress in the window, and sends it as MCP progress notifications to an agent that asks for them
Asking firstA switch each for seeing what plays, controlling playback, the sound or full screen, opening a file or closing a window, and editing or exporting

Technology

LanguageRust, edition 2024, Rust 1.95 or newer to build
Interfaceiced 0.14 with wgpu, and scramble-ui, shared with prev and triib
MediaFFmpeg through ffmpeg-next: the system's on Linux, FFmpeg 9 in the Windows and macOS packages; libass for styled subtitles; cpal for sound
Network audioThe atdecc, avb-lite, avb-mrp, avb-net and avb-stream crates; raw sockets through a small helper holding CAP_NET_RAW on Linux; on macOS, the BPF devices, which the installer opens, and Apple's AudioVideoBridging framework for the Mac's own entity
TranslationsProject Fluent
AI agentsAn MCP server through rmcp, relaying to the running plaa over a private local socket (a named pipe on Windows)
Assistantrig-core for Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Ollama and OpenAI-compatible servers; API keys through keyring (the Secret Service on Linux, the Credential Manager on Windows, the Keychain on macOS)
FontsRoboto Flex and Material Symbols Rounded, bundled
System integrationXDG desktop portals and MPRIS on Linux; Win32 and the System Media Transport Controls on Windows; AppKit, MediaPlayer and Launch Services on macOS, with a menu bar through muda
DependenciesAll open source, with licenses checked by cargo-deny in CI

License and privacy

LicenseGNU GPL-3.0 or later; the bundled fonts keep their own licenses (SIL Open Font License 1.1, Apache 2.0), and the Visual C++ runtime the Windows packages carry keeps Microsoft's
Sourcegithub.com/scrambletools/plaa, with the sources of the libraries the Windows and macOS packages carry on each release
NetworkNone of its own: plaa goes online only to play the addresses and web pages you open and to open links you click; on your local network it finds TVs and speakers as the Cast menu opens, and takes part in the AVB network you choose. No accounts, no telemetry
AI agentsOnly those you allow, through plaa --mcp; no bundled model. What an agent sees goes to the model it uses
AssistantTalks only to the model you add: a local model stays on your computer, and a cloud model gets what it asks to see. API keys stay in the system keychain
Code signingWindows releases are signed by Scramble Tools LLC, and macOS releases with Scramble Tools' Developer ID and notarized by Apple; see the code signing policy
