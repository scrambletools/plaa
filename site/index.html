plaa

Plays what you have, where you want it. Video, audio, streams and web video, trimmed, exported and recorded, on your screen, on a TV or speaker, or across an AVB network, by yourself or with your favorite AI agent.

Download plaa Source on GitHub

Free and open source under the GPL 3 on Linux, Windows and macOS. In 38 languages, including right-to-left.

What plaa does

Plays almost anything

Every format FFmpeg reads, decoded on the graphics card where it can, HDR shown as it should be, with the file's chapters, tracks in the languages you prefer, and subtitles of every kind, ASS styled as made.

Streams and web video

HTTP, HLS, DASH, RTSP and SRT addresses, and web pages such as YouTube's through yt-dlp when you have it, in the quality that fits your screen or the one you choose.

Trim, split and export

Cut a file into clips, reorder, turn and flip them, and export the result on your graphics card's encoder where there is one, or the sound alone, compressed or lossless. Nothing changes until you export.

Record

Sound from a microphone, the screen or a window with or without its sound, and a camera, as QuickTime Player's New Recording does, in formats you choose.

A visualizer for sound

A sound file shows its cover with a spectrum of what you hear or the file's waveform under it, or a scene in three dimensions that moves with the music.

Cast to a TV or speaker

Send what plays to a Chromecast, or a TV, speaker or program that plays DLNA, on your network, and play, pause and seek it from plaa's own controls and timeline.

An assistant beside what plays

Ask about what plays in a chat panel, and the assistant looks at it and plays, pauses, seeks or changes the sound for you. Use a local model that stays on your computer, or a cloud model from Anthropic, OpenAI or Google with your own key.

AI agents

Agents such as Claude Code see what plays and control the plaa you are using over MCP, only once you allow them, and asking before the steps you choose.

Floating controls

Material 3 toolbars float over the picture and slide away as you watch, or stay docked, in light or dark, in your system's accent color, picture in picture and full screen.

At home on each system

Media keys and the system's now-playing controls, a single running plaa that takes the files you open, Finder and Open With, and the Mac's own menu bar.

Network audio, built in

plaa is an AVB and Milan entity of its own on Linux, and uses the Mac's on macOS, so what it plays goes straight to the speakers, amplifiers and interfaces on your AVB network, in time with them.

Send what plays

A stereo 48 kHz stream on the network's own clock, which any Milan controller can connect, starting once a listener is ready.

Network Audio

The listeners that can play plaa's sound, each with one button, and how the one you choose takes its stream, kept up to date as other controllers change them.

Listen here

The talkers on the network, each a button away from playing on this computer.

On a plain switch

Where the switch has no AVB, plaa falls back to AVB Lite and sends each listener, up to five, a copy of its own, still in time with the rest of the network.

Beside triib

plaa shares the network's reservations with triib's entity service where it runs, and keeps network time with ptp4l where nothing else does.

Code signing policy

Windows releases of plaa are signed by Scramble Tools LLC with Microsoft Azure Artifact Signing, and macOS releases with Scramble Tools' Developer ID, notarized by Apple.

  • What is signed: only programs built from this project's own source code by its GitHub Actions release workflow: plaa.exe, the libraries it carries and the installers.
  • Committers and reviewers: the members of the scrambletoolsllc GitHub organization, who review every outside contribution before it is merged.
  • Approvers: the owner of the scrambletoolsllc GitHub organization, who alone can publish a release, and with it a signing.
  • Signing runs only in the release workflow, which signs in to Azure without a stored secret. Everyone on the team signs in to GitHub and Azure with two-factor authentication.

Privacy

This program will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or operating it. plaa goes online only to play the addresses and web pages you open, through yt-dlp for web pages, and to open links you click. On your local network it finds TVs and speakers when you open the Cast menu, serves the file you cast to the one you choose while it plays, and, when you choose a network interface for AVB, takes part in that network. When you use the assistant with a model you added, plaa sends that model your messages and what it asks to see of what plays: to the provider you chose for a cloud model, or to the server you gave for a local model. API keys stay in your system's keychain. Agents reach plaa only through plaa --mcp on your own computer, once you allow them. plaa has no accounts and sends no telemetry.
